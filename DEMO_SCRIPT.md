# BHUMI-INTEL: 5-Minute Master Hackathon Demo Script

This script is tailored for a live 5-minute pitch or jury walkthrough for the Smart India Hackathon jury.

---

### Timing Breakdown
- **0:00 - 0:45**: Problem Statement, Identity & Differentiation
- **0:45 - 1:45**: AI Research Assistant with Traceable Citations
- **1:45 - 2:30**: Spatial GIS Context & Multi-Criteria Layers
- **2:30 - 3:30**: Transparent Policy Scenario Simulator (Baseline vs +10% vs +20%)
- **3:30 - 4:15**: Evidence Graph Lineage Exploration
- **4:15 - 5:00**: Automated Policy Brief Generation & PDF Export

---

## Step 1: Problem Statement & Authentication (0:00 - 0:45)
**Presenter Speech:**
> "Good morning, respected judges. India doesn't have a shortage of land data. The real bottleneck is that this data is trapped across isolated silos—DILRMP revenue records, Bhuvan satellite rasters, court dockets, and academic papers.
>
> We introduce **BHUMI-INTEL: Evidence Intelligence for Land Governance**, built for the Ministry of Rural Development, Department of Land Resources.
>
> We don't replace existing systems. Instead, we provide the missing **evidence layer** that connects data to traceable policy briefs.
>
> In our top right, notice our Role Switcher. We are currently logged in as **Dr. Rajeshwar Sharma, Policymaker at NITI Aayog / MoRD**."

**Action on Screen:**
- Show the Executive Dashboard.
- Highlight the 5-year Land-Use Trends chart showing built-up expansion versus farmland loss.
- Point to the active demo badge (*"Prototype Synthetic & Research Data"*).

---

## Step 2: The Core Inquiry & Explainable AI (0:45 - 1:45)
**Presenter Speech:**
> "A policymaker asks: *'What evidence exists about land-use change and infrastructure development in Lucknow?'*
>
> Watch what happens when we submit this query. Our platform doesn't just call a generic chatbot. It executes a structured 5-stage pipeline:
> 1. Understanding the question
> 2. Searching our indexed repository
> 3. Analyzing multi-temporal LULC indicators
> 4. Checking GIS corridor buffers
> 5. Synthesizing the answer with 100% verifiable citations."

**Action on Screen:**
- Click the recommended query button.
- Show the progressive pipeline badges.
- Show the synthesized findings.
- Click on citation card 1: *“Impact of Expressway Corridors on Peri-Urban Agricultural Land-Use Dynamics (2024)”*. Show publisher, year, methodology, and verified status.
- Highlight the **Explainable AI** box: *"Why this answer?"*, Confidence score (High), and explicit limitations.

---

## Step 3: Spatial Context in GIS Explorer (1:45 - 2:30)
**Presenter Speech:**
> "An evidence insight is incomplete without spatial geography. We click *Open GIS Context*.
>
> Here in the GIS Explorer, we have selected Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. Let us inspect our layers:
> - The red zone represents the expanding urban built-up cluster.
> - The green zone represents contiguous double-cropped fertile soil.
> - The blue arterial line is the expressway corridor.
> - The amber polygon highlights critical hydrological drainage catchments prone to waterlogging."

**Action on Screen:**
- Toggle layers on and off.
- Click the expressway corridor line to reveal the popup: *"Right-of-Way Buffer: 500m Protected Zone Recommended"*.
- Point to the district spatial telemetry bar at the bottom showing exact square kilometers.

---

## Step 4: Transparent Policy Scenario Simulator (2:30 - 3:30)
**Presenter Speech:**
> "Now, how does a policymaker test an intervention? We open the **Policy Scenario Lab**.
>
> We do NOT use a black-box opaque AI that claims to predict the future. We provide a transparent sensitivity simulator.
>
> We set:
> - Baseline: Current 2024 status
> - Scenario A: +10% moderate infrastructure expansion
> - Scenario B: +20% aggressive logistics expansion
>
> Our engine calculates the trade-offs:
> Built-up area expands to 650.9 sq km, but agricultural land exposure drops by 101.7 sq km, and groundwater extraction stress rises into the critical zone.
> Every assumption and limitation is transparently documented."

**Action on Screen:**
- Move the Scenario B slider to +20%.
- Click *Re-Calculate Scenario Indicators*.
- Highlight the comparative bar chart and indicator table with color-coded risk flags.

---

## Step 5: The Evidence Graph (3:30 - 4:15)
**Presenter Speech:**
> "This brings us to one of our core differentiators: **The Evidence Graph**.
>
> In one single interactive lineage, we connect:
> 1. National Expressway Policy
> 2. Peri-Urban Empirical Research Study
> 3. NRSC Bhuvan Spatial Dataset
> 4. Expressway 5km Buffer GIS Layer
> 5. Agricultural Exposure Indicator
> 6. Multi-Policy Simulation Model
> 7. Actionable Decision Outcome
>
> Every single node is clickable and reveals the complete chain of custody."

**Action on Screen:**
- Click on different nodes in the flow.
- Show the provenance inspector drawer displaying metadata and source references.

---

## Step 6: Automated Policy Brief Generator (4:15 - 5:00)
**Presenter Speech:**
> "Finally, we click **Generate Evidence-Based Policy Brief**.
>
> Within seconds, the platform compiles an official 12-section briefing paper containing:
> - Executive Summary
> - Core Policy Question
> - Baseline Spatial Telemetry
> - Scenario Comparative Projections (2024 - 2030)
> - Governance & Ecological Risks
> - Concrete Policy Recommendations: Mandating a 500m Agricultural Green Belt and Transferable Development Rights (TDR).
>
> We click *Print / Save as PDF*—giving policymakers an actionable, publication-ready briefing paper."

**Action on Screen:**
- Show the rendered policy brief.
- Click *Print / Save as PDF* to trigger the clean printable view.
- Conclude: *"Existing platforms provide raw data. BHUMI-INTEL connects evidence. Thank you."*
